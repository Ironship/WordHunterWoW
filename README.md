# QuestWordHunter

WoW quests are full of the language you actually want to learn — but they fly by. You accept, scan for objectives, and the words are gone.

QuestWordHunter keeps the quest on screen as clickable text. See a word you don't know? Click it, add a meaning, mark it **Learning**. Next time it shows up in another quest, it's already colored. After a while you have a real vocabulary list from the game, not from a textbook.

The addon works on its own. Optional companions add an English text pane and a ready-made German dictionary.

## What you get

- Quest text you can click instead of retyping into a notes app
- Colors for **New**, **Learning**, **Known**, and **Ignored** so you see progress in the paragraph itself
- A meaning, a personal note, and the sentence the word came from
- A word list and simple stats (`/whw words`, `/whw stats`) — how many words, how often they come back
- A quiet **Ready for Known** hint after five different quests and two weeks. It never auto-promotes anything
- Separate databases per language, so German and French don't mix
- Windows you can move, resize, and theme (`/whw settings`)

Buttons stay in English. Quest text stays in the language you set in WoW.

## Optional addons

| Addon | Why install it |
|---|---|
| [English Quest Panel](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunterWoW-ENPanel) | English next to the original quest, so you can check a sentence without alt-tabbing to Wowhead |
| [German Dictionary](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunterWoW-Dictionary-DE) | ~74k German quest words already glossed in English. Click a word and a translation is waiting |

Both require this addon. This one does not require them.

## Start here

1. Enable **WordHunterWoW** in the AddOns list.
2. Set WoW's text language and the addon's **Target language** to the same thing (`/whw lang`).
3. Accept a quest or open one in the Quest Log.
4. Click a word, add a meaning if you want, save it.

`/whw` shows or hides the panel.

Supported locales: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil). Retail 12.1 (`Interface 120100`).

Everything stays on your machine. No uploads.
